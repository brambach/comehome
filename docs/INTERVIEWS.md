## Asking for interviews

Drafts for Bryce to send or change. Nothing has gone out. Good places to find owners: the Incline Village and North Shore lost-pet Facebook groups (people who posted a found update in the last year), the Pet Network Humane Society in Incline, and friends of the family who helped look for Boots.

**A post or DM to someone who found their pet:**

> Hi, I'm Bryce. My family's cat Boots was lost in Incline for 51 days last year and came home, and I'm building a free tool for the next family it happens to. I saw you got your dog back (so glad). Would you be up for a 15 minute call about what the first day was like? No pitch, I just want to get it right.

**To a shelter or vet:**

> Hi, I'm Bryce, I grew up in Incline. I built comehome, a free page for lost pets around the North Shore: neighbors get an alert when a pet goes missing near them and every sighting lands on one map. It has a live feed of open cases you could keep an eye on. Could I take 15 minutes of someone's time to hear what would make it useful to you?
